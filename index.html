Research Agenda

Current focus: PhD and RA positions in Strategic Management, centered on AI, institutional change, and organizational governance.
AI, Institutions, and Strategic Adaptation in China

My proposed doctoral research examines how AI adoption reshapes formal rules, governance structures, and professional routines inside organizations. I am particularly interested in when AI improves organizational capability and legitimacy, and when it amplifies risk or creates superficial compliance.

Research Interests

  • Institutional response to AI-driven change under competition, regulation, and state priorities.
  • AI and organizational governance, including controls, accountability, and decision rights.
  • Cross-cultural and Asian comparisons in AI strategy.

Methods and Data Strategy

  • Quantitative strategy: panel-data analysis and quasi-experimental logic when identification allows.
  • Text-based analysis: policy documents, annual reports, and managerial communications.
  • Qualitative strategy: interviews and process tracing to identify mechanisms.
  • Empirical contexts: Chinese organizations, with comparative extensions across Asia.
